Registration-based LAGC activities

These London Autism Group Charity activities run regularly and require you to register before attending. Choose an activity to find out more and book a place.

Fridays at Farringdon

A weekly neuroaffirming space where autistic adults can socialise, work, study or spend time independently.

📅 Fridays, typically weekly 📍 Farringdon, central London ✨ Also includes free Autistic Student Support and Yoga Sessions

Autistic Film Club

Monthly film screenings for autistic adults, followed by an optional group discussion.

📅 Third Wednesday of each month 🕐 5pm–8pm 📍 Artizan Street Library, Aldgate

Autistic Workplace Support Sessions

Free autistic-led employment mentoring and supportive group discussion for autistic adults.

📅 Last Saturday of each month 🕐 1pm–4pm 📍 King Square Community Centre, Islington

A monthly lunch for autistic women aged 18 and over, with food and a non-alcoholic drink covered by the charity.

📅 Monthly Saturday lunch 🕐 12pm–1pm 📍 Cittie of Yorke, Holborn
