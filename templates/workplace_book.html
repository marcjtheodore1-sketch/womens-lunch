Support on the day

There are always at least two LAGC volunteers at every session, alongside your mentor. They are there to host the session and offer employment guidance.

They aren’t able to provide personal care or one-to-one support. If that’s something you need during the day, you’re very welcome to come along with someone who provides it - a carer, support worker, family member or friend.

Monthly employment resources and tips
