What happens at Film Club

Our aim is to create a psychologically safe, autistic-neuroaffirming film screening environment. You can take part in the way that feels comfortable for you.

🚪

Arrive from 5pm

A volunteer will welcome you, show you to the room and give you time to settle in.

🥤

Free snacks and drinks

Snacks and drinks are provided free of charge. You are also welcome to bring your own.

👥

At least two LAGC volunteers

There will always be at least two charity volunteers on hand to welcome attendees, explain what is happening and support the environment.

💬

Short introduction

The person who nominated the film can explain why they chose it. A volunteer can do this if they cannot attend.

🎬

Watch the film

The film starts around 5:30–5:45pm. You can move around, use sensory items or step out if needed.

☕

Optional discussion

Volunteers facilitate a relaxed chat after the film. There is no pressure to speak. We finish by 8pm.

🚇

Public transport

Aldgate station is about 180 metres away. Liverpool Street station is about 500 metres away.

♿

Step-free access

The main entrance is step-free. Lifts are available, and the first-floor community room is accessible by lift.

🚻

Accessible toilets

Wheelchair-accessible toilets are available on the ground and first floors. The first-floor accessible toilet uses a RADAR key.

🅿️

Disabled parking

Disabled parking is available on Gravel Lane, approximately 62 metres from the venue.

Access and sensory needs

You can tell us about access or sensory needs, seating preferences, allergies and anything else that would help when you book.

Free snacks, drinks and sensory items will be available. You can bring your own too.
