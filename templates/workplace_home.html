How the session works

Come to either part or stay for the whole session. The environment is low demand, confidential and designed so you can participate in the way that works for you.

🧭

1pm–2:30pm

One-to-one mentoring

Speak privately with an autistic employment mentor about job searching, career planning, workplace issues, reasonable adjustments or another employment concern.

☕

2:30pm–2:45pm

Comfortable break

Free tea and coffee, sensory items and a comfortable seating area are available. You can also take time by yourself.

🗣️

2:45pm–4pm

Speaking Circle

A confidential, safe space to share and learn from autistic people’s work experiences. You can talk, listen, write thoughts down or contribute anonymously.

Who the sessions are for

You are welcome if you are autistic, aged 18 or over, and:

  • currently in work
  • looking for work
  • seeking CV or career-mapping advice
  • looking for advice about reasonable adjustments
  • considering future job options
  • wanting to share and learn from other autistic people’s experiences

What to expect

  • Free tea and coffee
  • Comfortable seating
  • Sensory items available
  • A low-demand environment
  • A supportive autistic-led team
  • At least two LAGC volunteers present
