🛟 If someone needs more support than we can offer

You are a host, not a carer. You're not expected to provide personal care, one-to-one support or supervision — and you're not expected to judge whether someone needs a carer. That isn't your call to make.

What to do in the moment:

  • Do what feels reasonable and kind.
  • Keep yourself and everyone else safe.
  • Don't take on more than you're comfortable with.

If someone has come with a carer or support worker, that person stays responsible for supporting them — it's fine to gently bring them back in if they've drifted away.

🚨 If anyone is in danger or needs urgent medical help, call 999. Do that first — everything else can wait.

A few things that help:

  • Check the bookings below before the lunch, so you know who's coming and who's bringing support.
  • Anyone attending with a carer has their carer's mobile number saved on the booking — you can call them during the lunch if you need to.
  • You're never on your own with a decision. Step away, take a breather, or ask another volunteer.
  • Afterwards, write down what actually happened — what you saw, not conclusions about the person.

Then tell Chris or Kirsty. They'll follow it up properly.
